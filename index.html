HOW SWIPER WORKS

A quick game of timing and movement

Swiper is a browser-based arcade game where you switch lanes to avoid incoming spikes. Learn the rhythm, improve your run, and explore challenges created by the community.

Read the complete How to Play guide →

Swiper is built around short decisions that add up over a run. The challenge is not simply moving quickly. It is recognizing an obstacle, choosing the safer lane, and giving yourself enough time to prepare for what follows. Practice helps you learn the controls before you take on competitive modes.

There are several ways to use Swiper. Practice is a place to learn the basic movement. Bot Play lets you test your reactions against a computer opponent. Quick Play searches for an online match when enough players are available. Community Levels adds player-created obstacle patterns, so you can explore challenges beyond the standard run.

Explore gameplay strategies and tips →

Learn the movement

Start in Practice to get comfortable switching lanes and reading obstacles. Build consistency before taking on a faster challenge.

Challenge yourself

Play against the bot or search for an online match when other players are available. Compare your performance and work toward a new personal best.

Create and explore

Community Levels lets players publish obstacle patterns and play levels made by others. Use the reporting tools if a player, level, or score appears to break the community rules.

Read community guidelines →

Frequently asked questions

How do I move between lanes?

Swipe up or down in the arena. On supported layouts, use the on-screen movement controls. Avoid switching too late when an obstacle is close.

Do I need an account?

You can try available guest gameplay, but online profiles, saved online scores, and some community features may require an account and an internet connection.

How do I report something?

Use the in-game report controls for players, community levels, and scores. Submit a clear, accurate reason. Reports are reviewed and do not automatically mean a violation occurred.

Where can I get help?

Visit the Contact page for account, gameplay, or privacy questions.

SWIPER COMMUNITY

Chat Rules

Keep Global Chat safe, friendly, and fun for everyone.

Be respectful

No bullying, harassment, hate speech, slurs, threats, or telling people to hurt themselves.

Keep it appropriate

No profanity, sexual content, nudity, explicit messages, or links to inappropriate material.

Protect your privacy

Never share your address, school, phone number, passwords, private account details, or live location. Do not ask other players for theirs.

No spam or harmful links

Do not flood chat, impersonate others, advertise repeatedly, scam players, or post suspicious links.

Moderation is active

Messages are checked before they appear. Rule-breaking messages are blocked. Repeated violations can lead to a warning, a 1-day chat suspension, a 1-hour website suspension, or a permanent chat suspension pending moderator review. Spam controls and slow mode also apply.

Need help?

Leave the conversation, block or report where available, and tell a trusted adult if someone makes you feel unsafe. Never arrange to meet someone you only know from chat.

By using Global Chat, you agree to follow these rules. Quick Words are available to guests; regular messages and voice require an account.
